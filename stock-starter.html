The essentials

Three ideas to know

Start with the building blocks. You can learn the rest as you go.

🏢

What is a stock?

A stock is a small ownership share in a company. If you own a share, you own a tiny piece of that business.

⚖️

Price can move

Stock prices rise and fall as people react to company results, expectations, and wider market conditions.

🌱

Think long term

Investing involves uncertainty. Spreading investments and giving them time may help manage some risks, but never removes them.
